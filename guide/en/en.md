    We choose one of the ways to upgrade one of the units (you can upgrade all units, but it will not have this effect).

# Chapter 2: What's with the money?

    We have 4 ways to earn money: The Forest, the line that your units follow, the mine, and the looting of caravans.

## Forest

        In addition to earning OU, we can also earn gold, but for this you need to be near any of the existing creep boars there at the time of his death, in a small radius, all players there will be poured a small amount of gold.

## Mine

    For more effective earnings, you need to use serious methods. We already have passive earnings coming from the mine, which can be upgraded in the same tab as the hunter is being upgraded. But we can also earn money in it on our own. If you look closely, you will notice that the miner is running to a certain point.:

<img src="file:///C:/Users/ABOBUS/AppData/Roaming/marktext/images/2025-09-23-18-20-39-image.png" title="" alt="" data-align="center">

    If you get close enough to this structure and have one of the following items, "Pickaxe":

<img src="file:///C:/Users/ABOBUS/AppData/Roaming/marktext/images/2025-09-23-18-21-42-image.png" title="" alt="" data-align="center">

    You can open its interface and start mining by clicking on the ore that appears every 1.5 seconds. Each type of ore yields a different amount of gold. Also, different types of picks have a coefficient that increases income from earnings in the mine (increases the amount of gold given out for "dug-in" ore).

## The Battle Line

    If you are not interested in helping your hunter or clicking in the mine, then just go to the line where the battles are taking place between you and the creeps of the bot. If you are around when the creep dies, you will also receive some gold and your income will be generated from this (gold is given for each dead ENEMY creep in a small radius)

## The caravan

    This is one of the most gambling variations of earnings in the custom. Here you will have to calculate the cost of your attack squad (the "Caravan" button in the menu). First, you form an attack squad (it will reset to zero after it leaves and you will need to purchase again), every 3 minutes your and enemy units will leave, and if you kill the entire enemy caravan, then all members of your team will receive some gold. Here you must allocate your resources wisely in order to make a profit.
